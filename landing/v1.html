How it works

Four steps from arxiv.org/abs/… to read.

i.

Install the extension.

Pin the walnut paper icon. PaperFlow only touches arXiv pages and PDF tabs — nothing else.

ii.

Open any paper.

arXiv abstract, hosted PDF, or local file ://. The reader takes over with a clean two-pane desk.

iii.

Bring a model key.

Paste an OpenAI, Anthropic, or compatible endpoint. PaperFlow stores it locally — never on our servers.

iv.

Read, ask, save.

Chat in the margin, highlight as you go. Every note & citation is one ⌘K away forever.

BYOK · privacy by default

Your keys, your models, your papers. Nothing routes through us.

We don't host model traffic. We don't read your library. The extension is local-first; sync is end-to-end and entirely optional.
